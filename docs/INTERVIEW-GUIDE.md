# Intake interview guide (owner) — 60–90 minutes

Fill `data/clients/<slug>/intake.json` as you go (`content/intake.example.json` is the shape). For every answer, ask for the concrete "how does that actually happen" — that detail is what makes the documents theirs.

## 1. Organisation (5 min)
Legal name, trading name, ABN, entity type, address, states. Who owns and runs it. Board or committee? Key personnel names and roles (director, quality lead, complaints officer, incident officer, privacy officer, WHS officer, rostering manager). If one person holds several roles, say so — that is fine and must be documented.

## 2. Homes (15 min) — repeat per home
Address, participants, ages/support needs in general terms, co-tenants and how they were matched, roster model, active night vs sleepover, who holds the tenancy/lease, SDA-enrolled?, any relationship between the provider and the landlord/SDA provider (related party?), house rules today, who does maintenance and how hazards are reported, evacuation plan exists?

## 3. Supports (15 min)
Medication: administer or prompt? Webster packs? Who records? Any PRN? Errors in last year and what happened.
Restrictive practices: any chemical, mechanical, physical, environmental restraint or seclusion? Authorised under the state process? Behaviour support plans and practitioner? Monthly reporting to the Commission?
High-intensity supports? Mealtime plans/dysphagia? Transport in staff cars? Handling participant money (shopping, cash)?

## 4. Decision-making and rights (10 min) — SIL outcome 1 & 4
How do participants choose routines, meals, visitors, activities? Communication supports used? Guardians/nominees involved? What happens when a participant wants to change provider — has it happened? Service agreements: do they mention tenancy? Are tenancy and support agreements separate documents?

## 5. Safety (10 min) — SIL outcome 2
Walk through the last incident: who found out, how it was recorded (software?), who decided if it was reportable, when the Commission was notified, how the participant was supported, what changed. Same for the last complaint. Risk assessments for each home and participant — exist? Emergency plans? Co-resident conflicts?

## 6. Workforce (10 min) — SIL outcome 3
Headcount, employment types, agency use. Screening: everyone in a risk-assessed role has a current NDIS Worker Screening Check? Where is the register? Induction: what does a new worker do in week one? Training: what's mandatory and how is it tracked? Supervision: who, how often, recorded? Shift handover: how, where recorded? How do you know practice is consistent across houses?

## 7. Governance and history (10 min)
Years operating; incidents, reportable incidents, complaints in the last 12 months; previous audits; existing policies (from where?); document control today; how policies are approved and reviewed; risk register exists?; continuous improvement log?; financial delegations; NDIS claiming process (who claims, how it's checked against rosters).

## 8. Registration (5 min)
Portal application started? Registration groups needed (0138; also 0115 for STA/MTA/ILO; 0104 high intensity?). Auditor chosen or quotes obtained? Target lodgement date. Who will be the contact for the auditor.

## Close
Confirm the review call date (Day 5). Explain [TO CONFIRM] flags and that nothing will be invented. Remind them: they must read and approve every document — the Commission requires the applicant to be substantially involved.
