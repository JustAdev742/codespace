# Deep dive: productized AI-assisted tender & grant writing (3 Sep 2026)

Summary of the research report (est. = estimate).

## Demand
- 27,147 tenders published AU/NZ in FY2024-25 (~2,260/month), down 23% from FY2021-22 peak; local government 12,106 (43.5%), from 500+ councils, no single portal.
- Top categories: commercial/industrial construction, building maintenance, engineering consulting, electrical, roads — exactly trades/subcontractor territory.
- Commonwealth 2024-25: 86,926 contracts, A$104.9b; SMEs win ~1 in 2 by number but only 14% by value (31% in 2021-22). Reporting threshold rose A$80k→A$125k (Nov 2025) so more RFQs, fewer small formal tenders.
- Grants Sept–Dec 2026 are thin for fast SME cash: EMDG Round 5 "late 2026" (closes within hours of opening → pre-prep product), Industry Growth Program (long lead), QLD funds between rounds.

## Competitors (published prices, AUD)
- The Tender Team: small tender A$1,000–2,150; medium A$2,150–6,250; A$165+GST/hr; smallest job A$700; 1–2 weeks, urgent 4 days; claims 66% overall / 95% incumbents.
- TenderWise: ~A$3,000+GST small, A$4,500+ larger; 50% deposit.
- TenderBuilt (civil): A$2,950–4,200 council; 7–10 days; 50/50; "founding client" discount (a new entrant using the same playbook).
- SaaS DIY: GovBid Pro A$349/mo; TenderAssist A$149–399/mo + A$499 setup; AutogenAI ~A$30k/yr.
- Capability statements: A$500–2,000+.
- Gap: nobody sells a published fixed-price, fixed-turnaround done-for-you small-tender product (except TenderBuilt, civil only). AI is neither differentiator nor liability yet.

## Willingness to pay / win rates
- First-time tenderers win 5–12%; average 15–25%. Consultants' 80–90% claims are selection-biased.
- WTP evidence is indirect (many consultancies, 50% deposits, volume claims). No forum complaints found.

## Acquisition
- Free lists of repeat winners: AusTender weekly contract-notice export (ABN, value, category), buy.nsw notices, QLD contracts directory CSV, VIC construction disclosures, ASIAL/BSCAA/Master Builders directories.
- Est. 300–1,000 SME ABNs listable per day; 100–200 contactable/day by hand.
- Spam Act 2003: inferred consent from a conspicuously published business email where relevant to the role; must identify sender + working unsubscribe.
- Benchmarks 2026: cold email reply 3.4% avg (5–10% good; small personalised lists 5.8%); LinkedIn accept 25–35%, reply ~10%.

## Delivery
- Small tender: 10–15 pages, 8–25 consultant hours; AI-assisted est. 4–10 h with a client pack; client must supply pricing, insurances, referees, declarations (turnaround gated by client).
- Productizable without a live tender: "Tender-Ready Pack" (capability statement + WHS/quality/environment policy set + case studies + CV/referee templates), 100% upfront.

## Risks
- No AU prohibition on AI-assisted responses; some agencies starting to require disclosure. Hallucinated facts in signed declarations = exclusion. Client fact-check sign-off required.
- December–January dead zone sits inside a 3-month window.
- Trust/sales cycle from an unknown solo vs incumbents with 20-year track records.

## Verdict (report's estimates)
- Best offer: Tender-Ready Pack A$1,200–1,800 (5 days, 100% upfront); Small Tender Response A$1,900–2,900 (5 days, rush +30%, 50/50); grants only for ≥A$20k programs.
- Reach in 2 weeks: 800–1,500 listed, 500–900 emailed. Conversion: 4–8 paid jobs in first 4–5 weeks. First sale: 2–4 weeks.
- Probability of A$32,500 within ~3 months: 25–35% (needs 15–18 engagements at ~A$2,000 through a December dead zone). More likely A$12–25k by then.
