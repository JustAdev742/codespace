# Candidate strategies (evidence summary)

All prices AUD unless stated. Sources listed in 02-sources.md.

## C1. NDIS SIL "Registration Sprint" (tailored policies + application drafting + evidence pack)
- Trigger: mandatory registration for Supported Independent Living (SIL) and NDIS digital platform providers from 1 Jul 2026; unregistered SIL providers must LODGE an application by **1 Oct 2026** or cease (criminal penalty). New registration group 0138 + new SIL practice standards module.
- Market: 36,641 SIL participants (Sep 2025), A$15.9b/yr SIL spend. Sector-wide ~245k unregistered vs ~21k registered providers (ABC, Dec 2025). SIL-specific unregistered count not published (deep dive pending).
- Buyers already pay: consultants A$2,500–7,000 (registration guidance A$2,000–3,500; full service A$4,000–7,000); template packs A$599 (Effective Policy), A$649–1,095 (Provider Audit Ready), A$3,700 mega pack (Provider360).
- Proposed offer: A$1,990–2,990 fixed price, 5–7 day turnaround, tailored (not generic) policies mapped to Core + SIL module, self-assessment responses drafted, evidence checklist, audit-prep call.
- Path to A$32,500: 11–16 clients. Deadline creates urgency; after 1 Oct, demand continues (new entrants, audits, 2027 registration expansion) but at lower intensity.
- Risks: crowded consultant marketing; credibility without sector track record; templates must satisfy approved auditors; owner must handle calls/contracts; 4-week window.

## C2. Productized AI-assisted tender & grant writing for SMEs
- Buyers already pay: A$2,200–5,500 per small tender (TenderWise ~A$3,000+GST small; Tender Team A$2,750–5,500), A$165+/hr; grants A$700–5,000; retainers A$3–10k/mo.
- Proposed offer: fixed-price A$1,500–3,500 per tender, 72-hour turnaround; "tender-ready pack" (capability statement + policy set) A$900–1,500.
- Path to A$32,500: 10–15 tenders. No hard deadline; demand is continuous but each job needs client input and a live tender.
- Risks: proving win-rate; AI-use disclosure rules in some tenders; lumpy; sales cycle tied to tender calendars. (Deep dive pending.)

## C3. AI receptionist / missed-call answering for tradies (reseller or own build)
- Buyers pay A$149–699/mo (+A$0–500 setup). Many AU competitors already (Fully Booked, AI Dial, Valory, Never Miss A Call, Hey Jodie, ReplyMate, Basic Solutions).
- Path to A$32,500 at A$300/mo: ~10 clients × 11 months, or 30+ clients. Too slow via recurring alone; setup fees small.
- Verdict: saturated, low ticket, slow to target.

## C4. AI automation single-workflow projects for SMBs
- Buyers pay A$2,500–5,000 per workflow; retainers A$300–1,500/mo. Highly saturated "AI automation agency" positioning; acquisition is the bottleneck; scoping/delivery per client is bespoke.
- Path: 7–13 projects. Viable as a secondary offer to existing clients, weak as the primary wedge with no audience.

## C5. Micro-SaaS / developer tool
- ~70% of micro-SaaS earn < US$1k MRR; median time to US$1k MRR is months. Fails the speed test for A$32,500.

## C6. B2B appointment setting / outbound lead-gen service
- Buyers pay A$150–600 per qualified meeting; retainers A$3–10k/mo. Requires deliverability infrastructure (domains, warm-up = spend), Spam Act constraints, and the result is dependent on the client's offer. Slow to prove, spend required. Secondary at best.

## C7. Property-management / real-estate automation
- Tooling priced per property (A$1.39–1.50/property/week); agencies budget A$2–5k/mo for automation. Long sales cycles with agencies; incumbents (PropertyMe etc.) integrate poorly for outsiders. Not fast.

## C8. Bookkeeping-firm document collection / practice tooling
- Pain is real (65% cite document chasing) but served by cheap SaaS (Content Snare ~A$35/mo, Financial Cents, TaxDome). Low ticket; slow.

## C9. Downloadable NDIS policy template packs (product, not service)
- Existing sellers at A$599–1,095. Instant delivery, scalable, but SEO-driven discovery takes months and incumbents already rank. Works as a lower-tier add-on to C1, not as the primary wedge.
