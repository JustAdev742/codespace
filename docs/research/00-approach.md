# Research approach

Goal: find the fastest realistic, legitimate path to A$32,500 AUD for a solo operator in Australia with strong software/AI/writing capability, ~zero capital, and owner approval required for spend, accounts and external actions.

Scoring rubric (1-10 each):
revenue potential · speed to first sale · startup cost (10 = near zero) · competition (10 = weak) · technical difficulty (10 = easy) · acquisition difficulty (10 = easy) · gross margin · scalability · risk (10 = low) · probability of reaching A$32,500

Weighting for the decision: probability of reaching A$32,500 and speed to first sale are weighted double; everything else single.

Areas investigated:
1. Trades / local services software & productized services
2. Compliance-driven niches (NDIS, aged care, Fair Work, WHS)
3. Micro-SaaS / developer tools / marketplaces
4. AI automation services for SMBs
5. Digital products / templates / gig marketplaces
6. Accounting & bookkeeping ecosystem
7. Property & professional services (real estate, legal, health, tenders/grants)
8. E-commerce, agencies, lead-gen services
