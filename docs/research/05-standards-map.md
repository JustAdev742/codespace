# Standards → document map (build reference)

Sources: ndiscompliant.com.au Core Module guide (2026), ndiscompliant.com.au SIL four-outcomes guide (updated 9 Jun 2026, draft wording), auditcore.com.au SIL guide (final, 1 Jul 2026). Verify final SIL outcome wording against ndiscommission.gov.au before publishing any client document.

## Core Module (certification audit; all SIL providers)
Group 1 Rights & Responsibilities: 1.1 Person-centred supports · 1.2 Individual values & beliefs · 1.3 Privacy & dignity · 1.4 Independence & informed choice / dignity of risk · 1.5 Violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation & discrimination.
Group 2 Governance & Operational Management: 2.1 Governance & operational management · 2.2 Risk management · 2.3 Quality management · 2.4 Information management · 2.5 Feedback & complaints / financial management · 2.6 Human resource management (incl. worker screening) · continuity of supports · emergency & disaster management.
Group 3 Provision of Supports: access · support planning · service agreements · responsive support provision · transitions to or from the provider.
Group 4 Support Provision Environment: safe environment · participant money & property · management of medication · mealtime management · management of waste.

## SIL supplementary module (from 1 Jul 2026, registration group 0138) — four outcomes
1. Supported decision-making — decisions made BY participants; ongoing supported choice about routines, relationships, home; dignity of risk.
2. Safety / safeguarding in the home — incident, complaints, risk, emergency per home, restrictive practices governance, co-resident risks.
3. Workforce competence & consistency / practice governance — induction, training, supervision, competency, consistency across shifts and houses.
4. Housing & support security / tenancy separation — service agreement separates support from tenancy; ending support ≠ losing housing; conflict-of-interest where provider is also landlord; transition/exit protections.

## Documents every SIL pack must contain (policies P, procedures Pr, registers R, templates T)
Rights: P Person-centred supports · P Diversity & cultural safety · P Privacy & confidentiality (+Pr consent, Pr privacy breach) · P Autonomy & dignity of risk · P Supported decision-making · P Safeguarding/VANED · Pr Open disclosure · Pr Mandatory & reportable incident reporting · P Whistleblower.
Governance: Governance framework · Delegations of authority · P Document control (+R Policy register) · R Conflicts of interest · P Risk management (+R Risk register, T risk assessment) · Business continuity plan · Emergency & disaster management plan (+ per-home evacuation plan) · P Quality & continuous improvement (+R CI register, Pr internal audit) · P Information management (+Records retention schedule, P IT security) · P Financial management (+R delegations, Pr NDIS billing & claiming, P fraud & corruption) · P Complaints & feedback (+R Complaints register) · P Incident management (+R Incident register).
HR: P Human resources · Pr Recruitment & selection · P Induction (+T induction checklist) · P Supervision & performance · P Grievance & disciplinary · Pr Staff separation · R Worker screening · R Training · Position descriptions · Contractor screening verification.
Supports: Pr Access/intake · Pr Assessment & support planning (+T support plan) · T SIL service agreement (support terms separate from tenancy) · Pr Responsive support / progress notes · P Transitions & exit (+T transition plan, handover summary).
Environment: P Safe environment / property maintenance (+R maintenance log, R hazard inspections) · P Participant money & property · P Medication management (+T MAR, competency assessment) · P Mealtime management · P Waste management.
SIL-specific: P Household decision-making & household rules (+household meeting record) · P Health & wellbeing (+individual health plan, appointment log) · Restrictive practices P + records (or documented non-use) · Conflict-of-interest disclosure for provider-landlord · Participant rights statement (accessible) · Shift handover template.

## Intake questions that drive tailoring (what makes it not a template)
Legal entity, ABN, key personnel; number of SIL homes, addresses/states, participants per home; roster model (24/7, sleepover, active night); who owns/leases the homes (SDA provider, provider itself, private landlord); medication involvement (prompting/administering); restrictive practices used (any authorised?); existing incidents/complaints history; workforce size, screening status, training currency; software used (rostering, notes, incident); current policies (if any) and their gaps; target registration groups beyond 0138; auditor chosen (if any).
