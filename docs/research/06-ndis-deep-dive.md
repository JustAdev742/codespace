# Deep dive: NDIS SIL mandatory registration (3 Sep 2026) — summary

## Legal position (confirmed from Commission/NDIA sources via secondary quotes)
- 1 Jul 2026: mandatory registration commenced for SIL (group 0138) and digital platforms (0137). SIL quality indicators: NDIS (Quality Indicators) Amendment (SIL) Guidelines 2026, F2026N00443.
- 1 Oct 2026: providers delivering SIL who have not lodged a valid application must stop. "Applied" = valid application lodged in the NDIS Commission Applications Portal (PRODA): org details, key personnel, registration groups, self-assessment against Core + SIL modules with evidence. Audit need not be booked by then. A started application must be completed within 60 days.
- NDIA payment rule: after 1 Oct plan managers must reject invoices from SIL providers that are neither registered nor applied; non-applicants can only claim to 30 Sep 2026.
- Penalty: 2 years' imprisonment and/or 120 penalty units = A$43,680 (unit A$364 from 1 Jul 2026); strict liability incl. sole traders; civil penalties up to 10,000 units.
- Platform providers: apply by 1 Oct 2026; extra conditions from 1 Jan 2027.

## Market size
- SIL: 36,808 participants, A$16.4b/yr, A$447k avg per participant. Sector: 270,381 active providers, 16,363 registered, 254,018 unregistered (ANAO).
- No official count of unregistered SIL providers exists. Estimate: 1,500–3,000 SIL providers nationally; several hundred to ~1,500 unregistered at 1 Jul 2026. Provider Link directory lists only 61 SIL providers. "Thousands" claims are vendor marketing.
- 2027: announced (not legislated) expansion of mandatory registration to personal care, daily living and closed settings from 1 Jul 2027; all providers in scope by Dec 2030.

## Application mechanics
Portal → self-assessment (Core 4 divisions + SIL 4 outcomes: supported decision-making; safeguarding; practice governance; agreements about tenancy, housing and support) → Commission issues audit scope → quotes from Approved Quality Auditors (19 remain; several exited 2026; "fully booked 6–8 weeks out") → certification audit Stage 1 desktop + Stage 2 on-site → non-conformities closed → decision. Audit cost single-site SIL A$7–15k; certification 10–16 weeks; end-to-end 4–12 months. No application fee.

## Demand signals
Real but thin and vendor-amplified: FB groups (NDIS Provider Forum, NDIS Provider Registration Forum, NDIS Providers & Participants Directory) with posts asking for registration consultants; LinkedIn engagement modest; consultation feedback: cost worries, lack of information.

## Finding & contacting prospects
- Verify status on the Commission Provider Register (check group 0138, not 0115). Sources: Provider Link SIL category (phone numbers; "registered" filter), MyCareSpace (lists unregistered too), Clickability, Google Maps, SDA/SIL vacancy sites (Nest, Housing Hub).
- Spam Act 2003 Sch 2 cl 4: inferred consent for conspicuously published business addresses where the message is relevant to the recipient's business functions and no "no unsolicited messages" notice; identify sender; working unsubscribe honoured within 5 business days. Phone calls to businesses are outside the Spam Act.

## Liability & credibility
- No licence required; avoid "legal advice" framing; no "guaranteed pass" claims (ACL s18). Standard disclaimers: does not guarantee registration/audit success; not legal advice; not affiliated with NDIA/Commission.
- Commission warning: applicants using consultants or purchased policies must be "substantially involved"; consultants providing "the same or very similar information" to multiple providers may indicate lack of understanding → refusal or extra audit conditions. Auditors interview staff and look for org-specific procedures and live registers.

## After 1 Oct 2026
Everyone who lodges still needs audit-ready documentation and Stage 1/2 audits through mid-2027 (auditor backlog); new SIL entrants (ongoing); registered SIL providers audited against the SIL module at next mid-term/renewal (whole registered base); platform conditions 1 Jan 2027; 2027 expansion if legislated.

## Verdict (report's estimates)
- Price: A$1,800–2,500 lodgement sprint + separate A$1,500–3,000 audit-readiness phase. Above A$3,000 collides with incumbents carrying guarantees.
- Reach in 4 weeks, zero budget: 150–400 identifiable candidates; 30–80 conversations; solo capacity ~8–12 sprints before 1 Oct.
- Probability of 10+ paying clients before 1 Oct: 15–25%. A$32,500 more plausible over Oct–Dec via audit-prep than by 1 Oct.
- Top risks: timing/saturation; regulatory (near-identical documentation trigger); market-size uncertainty.
