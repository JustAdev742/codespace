Shoot two linked portals onto the walls of a test chamber, then walk, fall and throw things through them. Switch between the video-game rule and an energy-conserving rule and watch the ledger.

This simulates a mathematical model: two discs of flat space glued together. It does not create, test or demonstrate a physical spacetime connection, and the study behind it finds that no such device can be built with known physics.
