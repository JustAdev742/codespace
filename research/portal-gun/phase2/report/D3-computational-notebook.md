## The experiments, one by one

### E1. Smooth-throat QI bound (analytic + numeric)

- **Model.** Reflection-symmetric throat; Ford–Roman QI (massless scalar, Lorentzian sampling); $f = 0.01$;
  observer speed optimised.
- **Equations.** D2 §5.1–5.2.
- **Parameters.** Flare-out $\varepsilon$ = 2, 0.5, 0.2, 0.05, 0.01, 0.003, 0.001. Two-scale family with
  $L = \max(20, 4/\varepsilon)\,r_0$.
- **Method.** Closed-form maximisation; numerically, 3000 Lorentz factors (geometric grid, $1 + 10^{-6}$ to $10^5$)
  at the throat, with five-point stencils for the variation lengths.
- **Validation.** The analytic maximum is at $x = \varepsilon\gamma^2 = 2$; the numerical optimum $\gamma$ agrees
  with $\sqrt{2/\varepsilon}$ to 0.3%. The sampled-integral version agrees within 0.5%.
- **Result.** $D/D_{\rm ref} = 1.0000$ for $\varepsilon \le 0.2$; 0.20 at $\varepsilon = 0.5$ and 0.25 for Ellis,
  where the variation length is shorter than $r_0$.
- **Limitations.** Flat-space QI applied in curved space. The variation-length prescription is a modelling
  choice, worth a factor ≤ 5.

### E2. Thin exotic region

- **Model.** Compact throat (exotic region of proper width $a$, flat outside).
- **Parameters.** $a/r_0$ = 0.3 to 0.01.
- **Method.** Full scan over position (±3 $r_0$, 241 points) × speed (241 points).
- **Result.** Deficit $\propto a^{3.0}$ for $a \le 0.05\,r_0$ (2.91 including $a = 0.3$). The tidal impulse for a
  walker is 0.96–1.00 m/s throughout.
- **Validation.** The Morris–Thorne thin band gives the same law. The analytic $a_*$ for 1 m is
  $9.2\times10^{-22}$ m.
- **Limitation.** The finite grid in $\gamma$ leaves the maximum uncertain by under 1%.

### E3. Redshift structures and the design search

- **Model.** Compact throat with redshift well or hill, of amplitude $|\phi_a| \le 3$ and width 0.01–3 m.
- **Method.** 400 random designs (seed 7). Constraints fixed in advance: crossing speed ≤ 0.01 c, tidal
  impulse ≤ 2 m/s, peak tide ≤ $10^4$ g.
- **Result.**
  - 120 designs pass, all with no redshift structure.
  - Of the rejects, 150 failed on a hill that needs > 0.01 c to climb and 130 on relativistic,
    high-tide crossing.
  - The deficit decreases toward the thinnest allowed region: minimum $D = 3.7\times10^{53}$ at $a = 1$ mm,
    the search bound.
- **Limitation.** Random search, not proof. The analytic argument of D2 §5.4 (C7) carries the conclusion.

### E4. Energy bookkeeping and the ANEC identity

- **Parameters.** Six families, with 400 001-point grids out to ±200 $r_0$.
- **Results.**
  - Tolman mass $< 10^{-14}$ for all.
  - ANEC direct vs identity agree to the analytic tail correction.
  - Ellis closed forms reproduced to $10^{-3}$, limited by the grid's tail.
  - The compact thin region tends to the thin-shell value $-2r_0$ (0.1%).
  - Two-scale throat: $+74.6\,r_0$.

### E5. Ellis–Bronnikov instability

- **Model.** Radial linear perturbations in the $\delta\phi = 0$ gauge (master equation D2 §6.1).
- **Methods and results:**

  | Method | Settings | Growth rate $s$ ($c/b$) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Shooting (DOP853, rtol $10^{-11}$, Wronskian at $x = 1$, outer boundary $X = 40$) | — | 1.1819727 |
  | Finite-difference matrix | N = 500–4000, $X = 60$ | → 1.182028 (second order; Richardson 1.181972) |
  | Chebyshev in $z = x^2$ | N = 100–220 | → 1.1819735 |
  | Leapfrog evolution with a damping layer | N = 3000, T = 40 | 1.18215 |

- **Validation.** The exact static solution $\delta R = R_0$ satisfies the equation to $10^{-12}$.
- **Limitations.** Linear and radial only. Non-radial modes not computed.

### E6. Thin-shell stability

- **Method.** Closed-form $V''(a_0)$ (D2 §6.4).
- **Results.**
  - $M = 0$: $\beta^2 < -1/2$ at all radii.
  - The minimum stable $\beta^2$ for $a_0 < 3M$ is $3/2 + \sqrt3$, at $a_0 = (3+\sqrt3)M/2$ (numeric scan:
    3.232051 at 2.366).
  - For $a_0 > 3M$ the bound rises toward $-1/2$ (−0.52 at 50M).

### E7. Delayed feedback

- **Model.** $\ddot q = s^2q - Kq(t-\tau) - D\dot q(t-\tau)$.
- **Method.** Pseudospectral generator with $n$ = 30 Chebyshev nodes; the rightmost root is unchanged to 8
  digits for $n$ = 10–60. Rightmost root minimised over $(K, D)$ with Nelder–Mead from two starts.
- **Result.** Stabilisable iff $s\tau < \sqrt2$:

  | $s\tau$ | Best rightmost root |
  |---|---|
  | 1.380 | −0.0174 |
  | 1.410 | −0.0021 |
  | 1.414 | −0.0001 |
  | 1.420 | +0.0029 |
  | 1.450 | +0.0176 |

### E8. Parametric driving

- **Single mode.** Floquet monodromy (DOP853). The threshold amplitude is within 0.4–5% of $\sqrt2s\Omega$ for
  $\Omega/s$ = 3–40.
- **Averaged operator.** No positive eigenvalue above threshold (N = 3000).
- **Full PDE.**
  - Runs: 15 configurations × 16 amplitudes (`kapitza_scan.json`), 5 × 14 extreme configurations
    (`kapitza_scan_extreme.json`), and a 3 × 10 refinement (`kapitza_refine.json`).
  - Result: no stable run.
  - Resolution test: N = 10 000 → 20 000, with CFL halved. The late growth changes (0.086 → 0.063 → 0.165 at
    $a/a_K = 1.09$), so it is **not converged**.
- **Limitation.** The drive term is a model, not a self-consistent oscillating background.

### E9. Chronology network

- **Validated cases.** Listed in D2 §8.1.
- **Scenarios.** Floor/ceiling, gun/wall, home/office, valley/mountain from the network checker. Orbit,
  Moon, Mars and star from closed-form constant rates at closest approach.
- **Limitations.** First order in $U/c^2$, $v^2/c^2$. Orbital cases use constant rates and minimum distances
  rather than integrated orbits.

## Known shortcomings

1. The QI audit inherits every assumption of the Ford–Roman method; it is a necessary-condition test.
2. `families.ThinBand` tabulates $l(r)$ on a grid; its derivatives in $l$ use the Schwarzschild-gauge
   formulas and are exact, but the interpolation of $r(l)$ is piecewise linear.
3. `tides.crossing` integrates on a sinh-stretched grid; impulses through bands thinner than about $10^{-4}$ of
   the grid span were not computed directly. The $10^{-21}$ m statement uses the analytic law confirmed down
   to $a = 0.01\,r_0$.
4. The parametric late instability is unconverged (E8).
5. Proper times in `chronology.py` use the trapezoid rule on the supplied grid. Discontinuous velocities
   cost ~$10^{-5}$ relative accuracy.

## Proposed but not executed

- Non-radial perturbations of Ellis, and a nonlinear evolution (would test the assumption that the radial
  mode dominates).
- A converged Floquet analysis of the full parametric operator (spectral in space, complex-scaled absorbing
  boundary).
- A QI audit of axisymmetric or rotating throats (hypothesis H1 in [D5](D5-breakthrough-hypotheses.md)).
- A particle-production calculation on an index-2 Morse spacetime (H6).
