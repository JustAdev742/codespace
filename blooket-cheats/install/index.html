Blooket Cheats — Installer

Drag a button to your bookmarks bar, or click Copy and paste into the browser console (F12) while on Blooket.

Two ways to install:
  1. Bookmarklet — show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+B), then drag any button up onto it. Click it later while on Blooket to run.
  2. Console — click Copy, open Blooket, press F12 → Console, paste, Enter.
Start with a Menu (top) for a full on-screen cheat list. One-click browser import is also available via cheats/Bookmarklets.html, and a Tampermonkey script lives in install/blooket-cheats.user.js.
