Why not just buy a template pack?

The Commission is watching for copy-paste

The NDIS Commission states that applicants who use consultants or purchased policies must have been substantially involved in preparing the application, and that consultants supplying the same or very similar documents to multiple providers may indicate a lack of understanding — a ground for refusal or extra audit conditions.

Auditors interview your staff

At Stage 2 the auditor asks your workers how incidents are reported, how medication is managed at Harris Street, who holds the tenancy at each home. Documents that don't match your real rosters, homes and software fail in the room, not on paper.

Template sellers themselves estimate 80+ hours of editing, portal work and evidence assembly. You have four weeks and a business to run.

Questions providers ask

Do I have to be fully registered by 1 October?

No. The Commission's transition pathway requires unregistered SIL providers to have applied by 1 October 2026; you can keep delivering SIL while the application is assessed. Not applying means you must stop.

What does "applied" involve?

A valid application in the NDIS Commission Applications Portal (PRODA login): organisation details, key personnel, the registration groups you deliver (0138 plus any others), and a self-assessment against the Core Module and the SIL supplementary module with supporting evidence. Once started, the application must be completed within 60 days.

Is this legal advice?

No. We provide document drafting and registration preparation support based on the NDIS Commission's published Practice Standards, rules and guidance. It is not legal advice and we are not affiliated with the NDIS Commission or the NDIA.

Will the auditor accept these documents?

Auditors assess whether your documents describe how you actually operate and whether your staff can evidence it. That is exactly why we write from an interview and keep you involved, but no one can guarantee an audit outcome and we don't.

What if I already have some policies?

Send them. We gap-check them against the Core and SIL outcomes and rewrite or fill only what's missing or non-conforming. The price is the same.

Do you use AI?

Yes, with a human responsible for every document. AI drafting is how we turn a 90-minute interview into a full tailored set in days instead of weeks; a person reviews every page, and you review and approve before anything is lodged.

What about the audit itself?

You engage an Approved Quality Auditor directly (there are 19 on the Commission's list and several are booked 6–8 weeks out). Phase 2 includes obtaining quotes and preparing you for Stage 1 and Stage 2.
