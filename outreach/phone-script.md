# Phone scripts

## Cold call — unregistered SIL provider (60 seconds to a yes/no)
"Hi, is this {{first_name}}? {{owner_name}} here, I work with SIL providers on NDIS registration. Quick question and then I'll get out of your hair: have you lodged your registration application under the new group 0138 yet?"

- **"Yes, done."** → "Great. Have you booked the auditor? [If not:] Several are 6–8 weeks out; worth locking one in. If you want a second pair of eyes on evidence before Stage 1, I do that as a fixed price. Can I email you one line about it?" Log: contacted → conversation.
- **"Not yet / working on it."** → "OK. The deadline is 1 October to have it lodged, not registered, and the application needs a self-assessment against the Core and SIL modules with evidence. I do a 5-day sprint: 90-minute interview, then your policy set and draft self-assessment written around your actual homes. A$1,990 plus GST. Would it help to do 20 minutes on a call tomorrow so I can tell you exactly what your homes need?" Book it or email the calendar link. Log: conversation → call.
- **"We're not SIL / we do drop-in."** → "Understood — if it's a few hours a day it's not SIL under the new definition. Sorry to bother you." Log and mark out of scope.
- **"Using a consultant."** → "Good. If they're slower than the deadline needs, I can take the documentation piece off their plate in 5 days. Mind if I email one line?"

## Discovery call (20 min)
1. Confirm scope: homes, participants per home, roster model, who holds the tenancy, SDA?
2. Medication, restrictive practices, behaviour support plans, high intensity.
3. Workforce size, screening status, software.
4. What documents exist today; portal started?
5. Auditor chosen? Date?
6. Explain the two phases; recommend one; state price; ask for the go: "If I send the payment link now, can you do the intake interview tomorrow at 10?"

## Objections
- **"Too expensive."** → "Compared with what? Template packs are A$1,400–3,700 plus 80–200 hours of your time; consultants quote A$4–7k and 4–8 weeks. And the cost of not lodging is stopping SIL on 1 October."
- **"Can you guarantee we pass?"** → "No one honestly can; the Commission and the auditor decide. What I can guarantee is documents that describe your actual operation, drafted responses for every outcome, and a review call so you know exactly what you're lodging."
- **"We'll do it ourselves."** → "You can. The application takes 60 days from when you start it, and you'll be writing 40-odd self-assessment responses. If it helps, I can just do the drafting and you do the rest."
- **"Send me something first."** → "Sure — I'll send the one-page explainer and my calendar. When should I follow up if I don't hear back — Thursday?"
