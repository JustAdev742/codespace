**1 October SIL registration: what "applied" actually means (from the Commission's transition pathway)**

Seeing a lot of confusion, so here's the short version for unregistered SIL providers:
- You do not need to be *registered* by 1 October 2026. You need to have *lodged a valid application* under group 0138.
- A valid application = org details, key personnel, registration groups, and a self-assessment against the Core Module AND the new SIL module, with evidence attached.
- Once you start the application in the portal, you have 60 days to complete it.
- If you haven't applied by 1 Oct you must stop delivering SIL; plan managers are told to reject invoices from providers who haven't applied.
- The audit comes after: get auditor quotes early, several are booked 6–8 weeks out. Single-site SIL certification audits are typically A$7–15k.
- Commission warning: if you use a consultant or bought policies, you must be substantially involved, and identical documents across providers can be a refusal ground.

Happy to answer questions in the comments. (I do registration document prep for a living, so take that into account.)
