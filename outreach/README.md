# Outreach kit — SIL Registration Sprint

Rules (Spam Act 2003, Sch 2 cl 4): email only conspicuously published business addresses; the message must be relevant to the recipient's business; identify the sender (name, business, ABN, contact); include a working unsubscribe honoured within 5 business days; never email an address marked "no unsolicited messages"; keep a suppression list. Phone calls to businesses are outside the Spam Act. Every message is sent by the owner from their own mailbox — nothing here is sent automatically.

Funnel logging: `tools/funnel.py lead|stage|paid` after every real event. No projections.

## Segments
1. **Unregistered SIL providers** (must apply by 1 Oct) — primary until 1 Oct.
2. **Plan managers & support coordinators** — referral partners (they must reject invoices from non-applicant SIL providers after 1 Oct; they know who is unregistered).
3. **Registered 0115 providers** — SIL Module Gap Analysis before their next mid-term/renewal audit (primary after 1 Oct).
4. **SDA providers** — their tenants' SIL providers must be registered; referral partners.
5. **NDIS bookkeepers / accountants / auditors** — referral partners.

## Files
- `emails-sil-providers.md` — 3-step sequence for segment 1
- `emails-partners.md` — plan manager / SDA / bookkeeper partner pitch
- `emails-registered-gap.md` — segment 3 sequence (post-1 Oct)
- `phone-script.md` — cold call + discovery call scripts and objection handling
- `posts.md` — Facebook group and LinkedIn posts (owner posts, never spam groups: one value post, one follow-up)
- `../tools/prospects.py` — prospect list normaliser and verification worklist
