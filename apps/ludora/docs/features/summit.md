# Summit Scramble (workstream: summit)

A new, original 3D mode: the class climbs one mountain at night, and right answers are how you climb.

| What a person now sees or can do | Where it lives | How it was verified |
|---|---|---|
| Hosts can pick **Summit Scramble** in the catalogue (arcade, 3D, 1 to 200 players, 5 to 8 minutes) with its rules, settings (mountain height, climbers to the summit, leaps, wind, ropes, roped teams, night climb) and three variants. | `packages/engine/src/modes/summit-scramble/meta.ts`, registered in `modes/index.ts`, `modes/catalog-data.ts`, `package.json` (`./summit-scramble`) | `npx vitest run packages/engine/test/modes.test.ts catalog.test.ts common-settings.test.ts variants.test.ts` (all pass) |
| Right answers give grip (a bonus on every third in a row, up to 6); a wrong answer holds the next question back 2 seconds while your Glim catches its balance, and costs nothing else. | `summit-scramble/index.ts` (`answer`, `steady:` timer) | `rules-summit-scramble.test.ts` "Summit Scramble: grip" |
| **Climb** (1 grip, always works) and **Leap** (2 grip, up 4 ledges 7 times in 10, slide back 1 when it fails). A leap averages 2.5 ledges in calm air and 1.5 in the wind, against 2 for two climbs, and the screen prints that average beside the odds. The chance is drawn on the server from the game's seeded random source, from the same number the button prints. | `index.ts` (`drawLeap`), `odds.ts` (`leapOdds`) | Rule tests: odds match the printed 70% and 50% within 1 point over 40,000 seeded draws; leaps in real games land about 7 in 10 over 400 seeded games; the averages test |
| Camps every tenth ledge: nobody slides below a camp they reached (leaping past one counts), and the first three to each camp score 40, 25, 15. | `index.ts` (`arrive`) | Rule tests "never lets anyone slide below a camp…" (60 seeded climbs, 50+ slips), camp bonus and leap-past tests |
| Wind about once a minute, warned on every screen 5 seconds ahead; while it blows leaps work 5 times in 10 and the Leap button says so. | `index.ts` (`scheduleWind`, `windWarn`/`windStart`/`windEnd` timers) | Rule tests "warns every screen before it blows…" and "draws leaps in the wind at 50%" |
| Ropes on two ledges of every stretch, visible from below: land on one to pick it up, throw it to pull the nearest climber below up two ledges (never past you); you score 15, they score the height. | `index.ts` (`placeRopes`, `ropeTarget`, `rope` action) | Rule tests "Summit Scramble: ropes" (3 tests) |
| The climb ends at the time limit (7 minutes) or when enough climbers reach the summit (3, or everyone if fewer); points are height, camps, ropes and a summit bonus by finishing order, sized so a summiter always ranks above anyone still climbing and an earlier summiter above a later one. | `index.ts` (`checkFinish`, `results`), `meta.ts` (`summitBonus`, `POINTS`) | Rule tests "the summit and the end" (5 tests) and "finishing order decides the top of the table" (40 seeded bot games with teams and churn, plus a hand-played worst case) |
| Variants: **Roped Teams** (each team shares one height, grip bag and score), **Night Climb** (ropes seen only 3 ledges ahead, wind with no warning), **Steady Climb** (no leaps, no wind). | `meta.ts` (`variants`), `index.ts` | Rule tests "Summit Scramble: variants" and a bot game of every variant alone and with churn; `variants.test.ts` |
| Practice bots climb, leap from camps, throw ropes and finish games, with 1, 4 and 12 players, teams, churn, a pause and a flashcard set; replays are deterministic. | `index.ts` (`bot`, `botDelay`) | `rules-summit-scramble.test.ts` "conformance" (8 tests) and `modes.test.ts` |
| Each player's device shows a 3D night mountain that follows their own Glim: switchback path of ledges, camps with tents, flags and lantern glow, two cloud bands, snow, distant ranges and a moon, visible wind streaks; hop, leap, slip, rope and camp/summit animations. | `web/src/features/room/modes/summit-scene.ts` (`SummitScene`), `summit-layout.ts` | Looked at Playwright/SwiftShader screenshots at 390x844 (day and night themes, high tier); `e2e/summit-scramble.spec.ts` (3D on) |
| The big screen shows the whole mountain with every climber and name (a crowd on one ledge gets one stacked list of names, in 3D and 2D), the wind warning, the race for the next camp, mountain news and a points board that moves at most every 2.5 seconds, so it reads in order between slides. | `summit-scene.ts` (`SummitHostScene`), `summit-scramble.tsx` (`SummitScrambleHost`) | Screenshots at 1366x768; axe on the host screen in the spec |
| Every action is an ordinary button whose name says the cost, the odds and where a slip would leave you; the picture's label says in words where you are, your camps, who is near, the next rope and the wind, and moves are announced once their animation lands. | `summit-scramble.tsx` (`SummitScramblePlayer`, `describe`, `useMoveFlow`) | Spec checks the label before and after a climb, the button names and the live-region announcement; axe (WCAG 2.2 A/AA) reports nothing on the player's and host's screens |
| With 3D off, a drawn side view of the same mountain (path, camps, ropes, every climber, you in gold, wind) stands in for the scene on phones and the big screen. | `web/src/features/room/modes/summit-side.tsx` | `e2e/summit-scramble.spec.ts` "the same climb plays with 3D off" and screenshots |
| The mode's screens are lazy-loaded, use existing sounds and the game music track. | `modes/registry.ts`, `music.ts` | `music.test.ts`; `npx tsx scripts/check-public-bundle.ts` passes |
| `/dev/three.html?scene=summit` and `?scene=summit-host` preview the scenes with fake climbers (`&wind=1`, `&night=1`, `&ledge=12`). | `web/dev/three.tsx` | Used for the art screenshots |
