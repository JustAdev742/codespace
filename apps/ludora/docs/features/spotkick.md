# Spot Kick (`spot-kick`): results

A new, original 3D game mode: a penalty shoot-out under floodlights. Design and rules are in
`docs/MODES_3D.md` ("Spot Kick").

| What a person now sees or can do | Where it lives | How it was verified |
|---|---|---|
| Hosts can pick Spot Kick in the catalogue (party, 3D, 1 to 200 players, 5 to 8 minutes) with its rules, settings and three variants. | `packages/engine/src/modes/spot-kick/meta.ts`, registered in `modes/index.ts`, `modes/catalog-data.ts`; `@ludora/engine/spot-kick` export | `npx vitest run packages/engine/test/{modes,catalog,common-settings,variants}.test.ts`: all pass, including Spot Kick and its 3 variants with 1, 4 and 12 players, teams, flashcards, churn, pause and seed replay |
| Right answers earn footballs (up to 3); a shot picks one of five places and placed or blasted. The server draws the outcome, the keeper's dive and how it looked from the seeded random source, with exactly the chances printed on the buttons. | `modes/spot-kick/index.ts` (`drawShot`, `shoot`), `modes/spot-kick/odds.ts` | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts`: chances add to 100 at every learning and guard level; 30,000 seeded draws per shot type within 1.2 points of the printed goal, save and miss rates; 1,600 shots in real games within 2% of the chances shown before each one |
| The keeper learns: a place shot at again gets saved more, and mixing shots up makes it forget. The buttons always include it. | `odds.ts` (`learn`, `shotOdds`), `index.ts` (`readKey`) | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts` "the keeper learns" (3 tests, including the setting off) |
| Three goals in a row puts you on fire: the next goal counts double; a miss or save puts it out. | `index.ts` (`shoot`) | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts` "on fire" (3 tests: double goal, doubles only after a run of three over 300 shots, reset) |
| House Match: two teams, team goals add up, the big screen shows the scoreline, and the keeper learns the whole team's habits. | `meta.ts` variant `spot-kick-house-match`, `index.ts` (`teamRows`, `readKey`), host `Scoreline` | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts` "House Match" (3 tests); scoreline seen in a real game screenshot (2D, light theme) |
| Keeper Duty: every shot is at a rival's goal in turn (never a teammate); each player sets and changes the two places their keeper guards; guarded places are saved 25 in 100 more often and shown on the shooter's buttons and in the 3D goal. | `meta.ts` variant `spot-kick-keeper-duty`, `index.ts` (`foeOf`, `guard` action), `GuardPicker` | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts` "Keeper Duty" (5 tests, including the save rate over 300 shots and the house keeper when alone); real game screenshot (3D, phone) |
| Golden Goal: level at the top at the whistle, only the leaders may shoot and the first to score wins; level after 90 seconds is a draw. Works for teams. | `meta.ts` variant `spot-kick-golden-goal`, `index.ts` (`levelAtTop`, `canShootNow`) | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts` "Golden Goal" (5 tests) |
| Bots play the whole game, alone or with a class. | `index.ts` (`bot`, `botDelay`) | `rules-spot-kick.test.ts` "bots finish a game" (1 and 12 players, and Keeper Duty with 6) |
| Player screen: score, footballs and streak over a floodlit 3D stadium where your Glim runs up and strikes, the ball curls or flies flat to where you aimed, Mitts the keeper dives, the net bulges, the crowd of Glims jumps and your Glim turns round to celebrate. Strength toggles and five place buttons laid out like a goal, each with its chances; the result line follows the picture. | `web/src/features/room/modes/spot-kick.tsx`, `spot-kick-scene.ts`; registered in `modes/registry.ts`, `music.ts` | `e2e/spot-kick.spec.ts` (3D on); screenshots at 390x844 and 1366x768, day and night themes, looked at |
| Big screen: the same stadium as a highlights reel from three broadcast cameras with each kicker's Glim and name, the table (or the House Match scoreline), the latest shots, a final-whistle countdown and golden goal banner. Results wait until each highlight has played. | `spot-kick.tsx` (`SpotKickHost`), `spot-kick-scene.ts` (`SpotKickReelScene`) | `e2e/spot-kick.spec.ts` checks the host's "Latest shots" names the kicker; host screenshots in 3D and 2D |
| With 3D off: a drawn goal mouth from behind the spot, with the ball's path, the keeper's dive, a caption and glows where the keeper has learned. | `web/src/features/room/modes/spot-kick-goal.tsx` | `e2e/spot-kick.spec.ts` (3D off); 2D screenshots on phone and host |
