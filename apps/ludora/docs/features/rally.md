# Lantern Rally (workstream: rally)

A new, original 3D mode: a night kart rally round a lantern-lit circuit, where right answers are the fuel.

| What a person now sees or can do | Where it lives | How it was verified |
|---|---|---|
| Hosts can pick **Lantern Rally** in the catalogue (arcade, 3D, 1 to 200 players, 3 to 6 minutes) with its rules, settings (circuit, laps, puddles and cones, team relay, grand prix, time trial) and three variants. | `packages/engine/src/modes/lantern-rally/meta.ts`, registered in `modes/index.ts`, `modes/catalog-data.ts`, `packages/engine/package.json` (`./lantern-rally`) | `npx vitest run packages/engine/test/modes.test.ts packages/engine/test/variants.test.ts packages/engine/test/catalog.test.ts -t Rally` (all Lantern Rally cases pass) |
| Right answers give boost charges (hold 3; a right answer at 3 boosts at once); **Boost** spends one for 3 seconds of speed; a wrong answer makes the engine cough (slower for 1.5 s, next question waits). A guess is a fair gamble: about even on four choices, worth it on two, not with no idea. | `lantern-rally/index.ts` (`answer`, `boost`, `steady:` timer) | `packages/engine/test/rules-lantern-rally.test.ts` "charges and boosts" (4 tests) |
| Three lanes: **Steer left** / **Steer right** (arrow keys on a laptop) move one lane as a quiet move (the room sends a pulse, not full views); puddles and cones slow you, a shield takes one hit, lanterns give a glow, a shield or a spare charge (60/25/15, drawn on the server and printed on screen); a kart close ahead in your lane gives a slipstream. | `index.ts` (`step`, `layoutItems`, `slipstreams`), `odds.ts` (`drawPrize`, `prizeOdds`) | Rule tests "lanes, hazards and lanterns" (5 tests): prize odds within 1.5 points over 20,000 seeded draws; no row blocks all three lanes over 40 seeded layouts |
| **Auto-steer** switch: steers round most hazards (not all), goes for some lanterns and spends charges, so the race plays by keyboard, switch, screen reader or answers alone; nobody else can tell who uses it. | `index.ts` (`autoSteer`) | Rule tests "auto-steer" (3 tests): hits fewer than half the hazards of a kart that never steers, but some; no `auto` in other views, the host view or the pulse |
| The server owns every position and speed: a fixed-step simulation advanced to the game clock by every hook, with a 500 ms tick; the same seed and inputs replay to the same race. | `index.ts` (`advance`, `step`, `tick` timer) | Conformance "replays from a seed" (standings, labels and game length equal) |
| Laps and finishing: lap times, a final-lap call, the whistle 30 seconds after the winner, the rest placed where they are. Finishing order decides the table whatever the answers: 200 points a place beaten, 100 for finishing, 10 per right answer (first 15). | `index.ts` (`lapDone`, `finish`, `racePoints`), `meta.ts` (`placePoints`) | Rule tests "laps, finishing and placing" (5 tests), including a seeded test over 3 seeds where later finishers and a non-finisher answer far more questions right |
| Three circuits with their own shapes and scenery: Harbour Lights, Lantern Wood, Rooftop Run. | `lantern-rally/track.ts`; scenery in `web/src/features/room/modes/lantern-rally-scene.ts` | Rule test "circuits"; screenshots of each on the dev page, looked at |
| Variants: **Team Relay** (team distances add up, scaled for team size, no finish line), **Grand Prix** (three one-lap races on the three circuits, points carried over, reverse grids), **Time Trial** (alone, against a ghost of your best lap; best lap is the score). | `meta.ts` (`variants`), `index.ts` (`startRace`, `bank`, `teamRows`, ghost samples) | Rule tests "variants" (3 tests) and the variant conformance runs (alone and with churn and a pause) |
| Bots drive (with auto-steer) and finish races; 1, 4 and 12 players, teams, flashcard-only sets, pause, churn and late joiners all play to the end; a player's view stays under 8 KB with 40 in the room. | `index.ts` (`bot`, `botDelay`, `onPlayerJoin`) | Conformance tests in `rules-lantern-rally.test.ts` and `modes.test.ts` |
