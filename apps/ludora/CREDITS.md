# Credits

Everything a player sees and hears in Ludora was made for Ludora: the Glims, the art, the icons
of the game modes, the worlds, the effects, the music and the sounds (rendered by `tools/audio`
from Ludora's own scores, D43). What follows is what was made by others and is used under a
licence that allows it. The app shows the same list on its Credits page (`/credits`).

## Sound

The music and effects are Ludora's own. They are rendered with FluidSynth using the MuseScore
General soundfont (MIT licence, with parts in the public domain or under CC0), as
`tools/audio/README.md` describes.
