# 09 — Manufacturing package: models, drawings, tolerances, processes (Mk II)

This is the shop-floor specification for **KESTREL K1 Mk II**. The engineering numbers come from the generated
files (`docs/generated/actuator_family.md`, `structure_checks.md`, `mass_budget.md`, `power_budget.md`) and from
research note R10 (Mk II actuators). Where this page and an older document disagree, this page and the generated
files win. **Read §11 (design holds) before you order any metal.**

**Flanges are friction joints.** The dowels locate; the bolt preload carries the torque. On the KA family the slip
torque is the designed overload fuse. On the KH-C25 and KH-C20 the slip torque is *above* the cycloid's momentary
rating, so the arm is protected by the drive-level current limits in §5.3, not by slip.

## 5. Gear trains

### 5.1 Planetary sets (KA family): Mk II tooth counts

All sets are simple two-stage planetaries, 20° pressure angle, zero total profile shift (suns below z17 get x ≈ +0.3,
planets the matching negative shift). **Plan A is the baseline tooth plan of docs/04 §5.2 and §7**; its KA-130 stage 2
is the K1 geometry checked in `structure_checks.md` (m1.25, z18, 18 mm face, Lewis SF 2.16) and drawn in
`ka130k__s2_ring_gear_z54_m1.25.svg`. Every set satisfies the coaxial condition, the equal-spacing assembly condition
(Zs + Zr)/N = integer and ≥ 0.6 mm planet-tip clearance, and leaves ≥ 2 m of rim between the planet root and its needle
bore. The K1 table did not: its stage-1 sun z20 / ring z50 with 3 planets gives (20 + 50)/3 = 23.3 (planets cannot be
equally spaced), and its z15 planets left 0.5 mm of steel round a Ø6 pin plus needles.

KH-14 on the same principle: 23 N·m / (50 × 0.106) = **4.3 A rms**.

Why the bearing change: in a cycloid the eccentric bearing carries roughly T_out/(N·E) of tangential load. At 160 N·m
that is ≈ 3 kN per disc plus the radial component. A 6802 ball bearing (the size noted in `cycloid_profile.py`) is far
too small, so the discs run on needle cages and their bore must be **Ø26 (C25) / Ø22 (C20)**, not the Ø22 modelled
(hold H6). Output pins are solid and doubly supported because a Ø5 pin inside an Ø8 sleeve would see ≈ 1.7 GPa bending
at 250 N·m.

**Manufacturing routes for the discs** (choose one; all need the measurement plan below):

| Route | Accuracy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Harden 100Cr6 blanks to 60–62 HRC, then wire EDM: rough + 3 skims, bore and output holes in the same setup, 2 discs stacked | profile ±0.005 mm, Ra 0.4–0.6 | preferred for a home builder; lap the profile and bore lightly to remove the 2–5 µm recast layer |
| B. Wire EDM 42CrMo4 Q&T, then gas nitride | ±0.010 mm after nitriding growth | press a hardened 100Cr6 sleeve into the bore as the needle raceway |
| C. Jig/profile grinding from the DXF (Moore-type CNC jig grinder or a gear shop with a cycloid program) | ±0.002 mm, Ra ≤ 0.2 | best result, specialist and costly |
