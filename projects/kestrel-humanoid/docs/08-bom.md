## How to read it

Every line records:
- a **stable ID**, manufacturer and part number, quantity, unit price in the seller's currency, AUD conversion;
- **make/buy class**: buy, machine (CNC job shop), print (MJF), custom (outsourced to a drawing), make (you fabricate);
- **price status**:
  - **V**: price seen on a manufacturer/retailer page. Mk II additions were checked on 2026-11-09 and their URL is in
    the source column; older V prices come from research notes R1–R6;
  - **Q**: quote-only part, so the price is an estimate;
  - **E**: engineering estimate. Every custom part, and every bought part whose price page could not be retrieved this
    session, is marked E;
- the Australian supplier or source, an alternative part, and whether it is **baseline** (needed) or an **option**;
- **Mk II change**: new, changed, or unchanged against Mk I.

FX assumptions (AUD per unit): USD 1.52, EUR 1.65, GBP 1.95, CHF 1.75, CNY 0.21. **Update before ordering.**

**ID policy.** IDs never change, because the construction tutorial (docs/10) and other documents cite them:
- B001–B085 keep the slot they had in Mk I. Where Mk II replaced the part, the slot now holds the replacement:
  - B003: KA-110P motor, now RI115-PH;
  - B004: RI85-PH, 11 → 7;
  - B005: shoulder motor, now KM-115x32-N50;
  - B010/B011: shoulder and elbow reducers, now C25/C20 cycloids;
  - B024: wrist motor, now ECX FLAT 32 L;
  - B025: knee/hip drive, now HC2;
  - B028–B032: battery, now two hot-swap packs.
- B001 and B002 now hold the shared KM-130x50-LC lamination stacks and magnet sets (knee and hip pitch use one motor).
- Mk II additions start at B101.

## Alternatives built into the BOM

* **Knee/hip drive:** Elmo Platinum Twitter R70/100 (99 A) instead of HC2. It is buyable and SIL3, but the knee loses
  ≈ 30 % of peak torque (R10 §1.2). Use it for a Phase-0 mule only.
* **Arm reducers:** Leaderdrive / Harmonic Drive strain-wave (Mk I) if the machined cycloids fail their dyno tests. The
  catch is that the strain-wave gear limits the peak (127 / 73 N·m).
* **Motors:**
  - TQ-RoboDrive ILM kits instead of the scratch KM-115/KM-85 motors (Q prices, 10–12 weeks);
  - scratch KM motors instead of CubeMars RI115/RI85, using the R10 KA-115R / KA-90P2 designs.
* **BMS:** Orion BMS 2 per pack is possible on a bench pack, but too large to live inside a 5.9 kg hot-swap pack.
* **Power path:** per-pack contactors instead of the solid-state OR/tie paths. They are heavier and lose seamless OR-ing
  (docs/05 §4.6).
* **Encoders:** MT6835 magnetic instead of AksIM-2 on the motor side (already used on 19 joints).
* **Hands:** Inspire RH56DFTP (option B062). It misses the Mk II 13-DOA and 200 N grasp targets.
* **F/T sensors:** Bota SensONE T5 / PixONE T60 (options B056/B057) instead of estimating wrench from joint torques.
* **Cooling:** the price-verified Koolance PMP-420 pump (US$83.36), HX-720 aluminium radiator (US$69.60) and LIQ-705
  coolant are listed as alternatives on B119, B120 and B122. They miss the docs/03 mass, power or freeze spec, so use them
  for the bench loop. A custom micro-channel aluminium radiator saves mass (docs/05 §13).

## Small parts are included

Fasteners (12.9, A4, Ti), dowels, thread-locker, thermal epoxy, grease, connectors, wire, crimps, labels, filters,
bumpers, soles, fingertip caps, O-rings and a 10 % spares allowance all have BOM lines. If you find a part on a drawing
that has no BOM line, it falls under B081 (fasteners & consumables). Add it to `calc/kestrel/bom.py` with a new ID
(next free number above B135; B114 and B132 are retired and must not be reused) so the next roll-up is complete.
